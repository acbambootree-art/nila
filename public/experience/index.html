The Crown in the Indigo

A legend from the Malay Annals, told in nine panels.

Scroll to begin

nīla

Sanskrit · deep indigo

The colour of the sea between Sumatra and Singapore. Also the heart of a prince’s name: Sang Nila Utama. This is his crossing, and what we believe became of his crown.

Palembang

IPalembang

Long before this island had a name, a prince set sail from Palembang.

Bintan

IIBintan

On Bintan, he followed a stag into the mist. It vanished.

Temasek

IIIThe Great Rock

From a great rock, he saw a shore of white sand, bright as cloth.

Crossing

IVThe Crossing

He set his course for it.

Storm

VThe Storm

The sea rose against him. His men threw everything overboard. It was not enough.

So he lifted the crown from his head, and gave it to the sea.

Still

VIThe Calm

And the storm went still.

Singapura

VIISingapura

He came ashore, and named the place Singapura.

Indigo

VIIIThe Indigo

But the crown was never found. Seven hundred years in the indigo dark.Here the Annals fall silent. The rest of the legend is ours.
